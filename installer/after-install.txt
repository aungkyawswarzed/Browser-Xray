Finish Browser Xray setup in your browser:

1. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions). The installer can open
   this page for you.
2. Turn on Developer mode.
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose:
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\BrowserXray\extension
4. If Browser Xray is already in the list, click Reload.
5. Pin Browser Xray, open its popup, add a vless://, vmess://, trojan://, or
   ss:// share link, and press Connect.

The extension ID must be:
jmopdjjejcocokfkcjjpalhadkdmjklc

Uninstall from the Start Menu ("Uninstall Browser Xray") or Settings >
Apps > Installed apps. Remove the extension separately from the browser's
extensions page. Saved profiles are stored by the browser extension and are
removed with the extension.
